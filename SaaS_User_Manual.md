## 3. The Live Network Organization Tree

Our state-of-the-art Organizational Matrix allows administrators to visually manage company structures, regardless of size or depth.

### How to Use the Matrix
1. **Expanding Branches:** Click on any node to expand it. The matrix features **Intelligent Viewport Auto-Centering**, ensuring that the node you click smoothly pans to the center of your screen without being blocked by side menus.
2. **Data Discovery Tooltips:** Hover over any employee or department node. A rich, dynamic tooltip will appear instantly, revealing cross-referenced data such as current status, assigned roles, and key departmental metrics.
3. **Zoom & Pan:** Click and drag the canvas to explore different remote branches of your organization effortlessly.

## 4. Project Governance & Timesheet Controls

Managing projects and logging hours is streamlined and secured against outdated data entry.

### Managing the Project Registry
- **Interactive Accordions:** Navigate to the **Projects** tab. Here, you'll find an elegantly nested accordion list. You can expand sectors to view hundreds of concurrent projects neatly.
- **Decommissioned Projects:** Projects that have expired are highlighted appropriately. While you can view them for historical and audit tracking, the system's backend strictly prevents them from being selected in new active timesheets.

### Submitting Timesheets
1. Go to the **Timesheets** module.
2. Select your active project from the dropdown. Only current, validated projects will be available to you.
3. Enter your hours and submit for matrix manager approval.
